SAFETY PROCEDURES - MANUFACTURING FLOOR

Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) Procedure:
Before performing any maintenance on machines MCH-01 through MCH-10:
1. Notify the shift supervisor
2. Turn off the main power switch
3. Apply your personal lock and tag to the power disconnect
4. Verify zero energy state by attempting to start the machine
5. Only then proceed with maintenance work

PPE Requirements:
- Safety glasses: Always required on the manufacturing floor
- Steel-toed boots: Required in all production areas
- Insulated gloves: Required when working on electrical components or torque sensors
- Hearing protection: Required in the stamping area (above 85 dB)

Emergency Contacts:
- Floor supervisor: Extension 4501
- Safety officer: Extension 4502
- Medical emergency: Dial 112 (EU) or 911 (US)
